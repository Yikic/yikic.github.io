Cloning Myself(Speech Style)

I’ve always been fascinated by the idea of digital self-preservation, and with my custom computer set up and ready, I decided to take a creative leap. I got an idea: why not train a language model to mimic the way I speak, especially when chatting with my friends? So, I fine-tuned ChatGLM3 using my WeChat conversations, and to my surprise, it started replying almost exactly like me—about 90% on point (laughs).

To put it to the test, I invited a friend over to see if the model could really capture my style. The result? We both ended up laughing a lot. Here’s a quick demo of the conversation between my friend and “I”:

And I’m not stopping there! I’m also excited about cloning my voice – like the project “Chat with Joker Xue”. The plan is for the model to reply with either a text message or a voice note at random, adding a fun twist to the interaction (think of it as a playful bonus from a random distribution).
